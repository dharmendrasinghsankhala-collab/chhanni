# Lighting

## Core rules (scoring + suggestions)
- Key direction matters more than key brightness: a 30–45° side key gives shape; flat front light reads "news/ID photo".
- Contrast ratio guide: 2:1 corporate/ad-bright, 4:1 drama/documentary interview, 8:1+ noir/night.
- Soft source = big relative to subject and close. Moving a softbox closer makes it softer AND faster fall-off.
- Eyes need a catchlight; a shot with dead eyes on a hero close-up should lose points.
- Background ~1–2 stops under the subject separates them without extra lights.
- Window light: subject at 45° to window, negative fill (black cloth) on the far side for documentary interviews.
- Golden hour = warm, low, long shadows; blue hour = cool ambient + warm practicals — mark both as "hero" candidates.
- Harsh midday: find open shade or backlight the subject and expose for skin.
- Mixed colour temps are fine if intentional (warm practical vs. cool ambient); accidental green/magenta spikes from cheap LEDs/tubelights are a fault.

## Night / practicals
- Make practicals (bulbs, lamps, signs) the motivated key; use soft LED only as a low base layer.
  ([AC – Motor City, Nov 2026](https://theasc.com/article/motor-city-cinematography-matysiak/))
- Run neon / RGB tubes at ~30–40% so they spill realistically on wet roads, cars and skin instead of clipping.
  (same source)
- Light streets in uneven pools so subjects walk through light and fall-off — even coverage kills night mood. (same source)

## Warm/cold split (drama, music video)
- Camera at ~3800K, warm sources at ~2700K, "moonlight" at ~6000K gives a clean warm/cool split straight from camera.
  ([AC – Verity, Nov 2026](https://theasc.com/article/verity-cinematography-frohna/))
- Keep foreground vs. background ~5 stops apart on set, then widen the gap in the grade. (same source)
- Single-source eye light ("Obie"): small fixture right above the lens, lightly diffused. (same source)

## Flash + LED mix (portraits, dance, wedding)
- Set the least flexible light first (the sun, a fixed practical), the most flexible one last.
  ([Fstoppers, Nov 2026](https://fstoppers.com/editorial/how-i-shot-three-completely-different-portraits-profotos-essential-hybrid-kit-905308))
- Match flash and LED colour (gel or set the LED Kelvin) so they blend. (same source)
- Drag the shutter (1–2 s with ND) + flash to freeze the face while fabric/dupatta streaks; shorten if the face ghosts. (same source)
