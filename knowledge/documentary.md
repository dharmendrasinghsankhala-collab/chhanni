# Documentary

- Story first: find the character's want and the obstacle; select footage that shows change, not just activity.
- Interviews: eye-line just off lens, 2/3 framing, background with depth and a hint of the subject's world.
- Record 30–60 s room tone at every location; it saves the edit.
- Shoot sequences, not shots: wide (where), medium (what), close (detail/emotion), plus an exit shot.
- Hands, faces, tools: detail B-roll is what lets you cut interviews down.
- Observational scenes beat narrated ones; keep moments where nothing is "said" but something changes.
- Prefer natural/motivated light; add only negative fill and a soft bounce for interviews.
- Don't review on camera during an intimate moment — it breaks the connection with the subject.
  ([Fstoppers – Platon](https://fstoppers.com/education/why-platon-wont-show-presidents-back-his-camera-905501))
- Scoring hints: steady, well-exposed observational moments with clear faces and clean audio rank highest;
  shaky "searching" moves between subjects rank lowest.
