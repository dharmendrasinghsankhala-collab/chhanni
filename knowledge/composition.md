# Composition

## Core rules (scoring)
- Subject placement: rule of thirds or strong centre symmetry — "slightly off-centre" with no intent scores lower.
- Eyes on the upper third line in close-ups; give look-room in the direction the subject faces.
- Head-room: tight but not cut at the chin; cutting the top of the head in an ECU is fine, cutting the chin is not.
- Don't crop at joints (wrist, knee, ankle) — crop between them. Critical for dance (hands and feet carry the form).
- Clean edges: bright spots or poles growing out of heads are faults.
- Foreground layer adds depth; shoot through doorways, flowers, crowd shoulders.
- Leading lines and frames-within-frames are strong documentary tools.
- Horizon level unless a dutch angle is clearly intended.

## Framing by story
- Frame tight when the eyes carry the story; wide when hands or posture do.
  ([Fstoppers – Platon, Nov 11 2026](https://fstoppers.com/education/why-platon-wont-show-presidents-back-his-camera-905501))
  → For dance: tight for abhinaya (expression), wide/medium for nritta (pure movement) and mudras with full arms.
- Keep the setup fixed so the person is the only variable when shooting a portrait series. (same source)
- Split diopter (+1 on a 75mm) keeps a near subject and a far figure both in play — tension/"being watched".
  ([AC – Verity](https://theasc.com/article/verity-cinematography-frohna/))
