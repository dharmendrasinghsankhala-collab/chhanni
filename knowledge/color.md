# Color

## Core rules
- Balance first, look second: neutralise white balance and exposure per shot, then apply the look across the scene.
- Skin tones sit on the vectorscope skin line (~11 o'clock). Indian skin is often warmer/denser — protect it, don't push it orange.
- Keep skin in a consistent IRE band across a scene; a jump between matching shots is a colour fault.
- Shadows cool + highlights warm = classic depth; reverse only on purpose.
- Saturation: push the colour that tells the story (haldi yellow, mehendi green, sindoor red), hold the rest back.
- Avoid clipping chroma on LEDs/neon — reds and blues clip first on Rec.709.
- Night: a slightly cool, low-saturation base with warm practicals reads more "real" than overall blue.

## Tools / techniques
- Lightroom Classic 16 / Lightroom 10 (Nov 2026) add a Vectorscope with a skin-tone line and Colour Grading inside masks —
  use the skin line to match skin across a mixed-light wedding set.
  ([Newsshooter, Nov 10 2026](https://www.newsshooter.com/2026/11/10/adobe-releases-lightroom-classic-16-0-and-lightroom-10-0-glow-vectorscope-ai-depth-masks-and-tethering-on-lightroom-desktop/))
- Lightroom 16 also adds Glow (bloom, diffusion, halation) — use lightly; heavy halation on Indian bridal reds smears detail.
- Premiere Pro 27 (Nov 10 2026) replaces Lumetri with Color Mode: clip grid, sequence-level grades, colour reference markers.
  ([ProVideo Coalition](https://www.provideocoalition.com/adobe-ships-premiere-27-with-color-mode/))
- Resolve 21.2 (Nov 2026) adds ResolveFX False Color for Gen 5/6 — good for checking skin exposure on set footage.
  ([Newsshooter](https://www.newsshooter.com/2026/11/04/blackmagic-design-davinci-resolve-21-2/))

## Flat-light depth (landscape, outdoor wedding, documentary establishing shots)
- Brush ~+0.5 EV along the top of the frame to fake a light direction.
  ([Fstoppers, Nov 12 2026](https://fstoppers.com/education/4-editing-tips-give-flat-landscape-photos-more-depth-905509))
- Add contrast to the foreground, lift background shadows so distance recedes. (same source)
- Cool shadows/mids, keep highlights warm; adjust only hue strength, not hue angle, to stay natural. (same source)
