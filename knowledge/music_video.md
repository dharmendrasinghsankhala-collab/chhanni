# Music video

- Cut on downbeats for energy, on bar starts for phrasing; not every beat — over-cutting tires the eye.
- Match cut density to song sections: verse calmer, chorus faster, bridge breaks the pattern.
- Performance + narrative: alternate lip-sync coverage with story beats; return to performance on hooks.
- Lip-sync: check sync on hard consonants (p, b, m) — a 1–2 frame slip is visible there.
- Speed ramps land on beats; start/end ramps on accents, not mid-phrase.
- Strong colour concept per section (warm/cool split, single neon colour) gives identity.
- Night/neon: practicals as key, neon at ~30–40% so it spills on surfaces.
  ([AC – Motor City](https://theasc.com/article/motor-city-cinematography-matysiak/))
- Warm/cool split straight in camera (3800K camera, 2700K warm, 6000K "moon").
  ([AC – Verity](https://theasc.com/article/verity-cinematography-frohna/))
- Beat tracking: beat_this (downbeats) + allin1 (song structure) are the best free local tools (see models_catalog.json).
