# Wedding photography (Indian)

## Must-have moments
- Haldi (yellow, splashes, laughter), mehendi (hands detail + bride's expression), sangeet (dance, candid crowd),
  baraat (groom on ghodi, dhol, energy), jaimala, pheras (fire light on faces), kanyadaan, sindoor, vidaai (emotion).
- Family portraits: everyone's eyes open, nobody cut at frame edge; shoot 3–5 frames per group.

## Light & colour
- Pheras: firelight is warm and low — expose for faces, let the background go dark.
- Mixed light (LED walls, tubelights, flash): fix skin first. Lightroom 16's Vectorscope skin-tone line helps keep skin
  consistent across the set.
  ([Newsshooter, Nov 10 2026](https://www.newsshooter.com/2026/11/10/adobe-releases-lightroom-classic-16-0-and-lightroom-10-0-glow-vectorscope-ai-depth-masks-and-tethering-on-lightroom-desktop/))
- Match flash colour to ambient (gel CTO for tungsten venues).
- Reds (lehenga, sindoor) clip easily — watch the red channel.
- Lightroom 16 AI depth-range masks: separate couple from busy pandal background without manual brushing.

## Culling rules
- Reject closed eyes on the main subject, awkward mid-blink expressions, blur on faces.
- Keep candid emotional frames even if technically imperfect (vidaai tears, parents' reactions).
- Prefer variety: don't keep 10 near-identical frames; best 1–2 per burst.
