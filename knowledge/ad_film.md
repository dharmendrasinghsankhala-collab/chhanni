# Ad film

- Product must be clean, in focus, correctly coloured — brand colour accuracy beats "look".
- First 2–3 seconds must hook (movement, face, or a question); vertical cuts for reels need the hook in frame centre.
- Hero product shot: controlled reflections (large soft source, black flags for edge definition).
- Keep a consistent, brighter look (2:1–3:1 contrast) unless the brand is premium/moody.
- Leave room for supers/logo: clean negative space in end-frame.
- Deliver multiple lengths (30/15/6 s) — select shots that survive the 6 s cut.
- Mixed flash/LED: match colour temperatures so the product doesn't shift colour.
